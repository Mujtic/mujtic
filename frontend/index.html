Our Story

Built for Ghana's Vibrant Event Scene

Mujtic connects event creators with audiences across Ghana — from Accra's music festivals to Kumasi's cultural celebrations.

Where we started

Ghana's events deserve better than screenshots and cash at the gate.

Mujtic began with a simple frustration: brilliant events were being held back by clumsy ticketing. Tickets were photocopied, payments were chased through scattered chats, and organisers waited days to see their money.

We built Mujtic around how Ghana actually pays and plans: Mobile Money first, QR tickets that work on any phone, and payouts that are fast and transparent. No hidden steps, no guesswork.

Today, organisers sell tickets, manage gate staff, validate entry in real time and track revenue from a single dashboard, while attendees get a ticket they can trust in seconds.

“If an event is worth hosting, it should be effortless to sell out.”
  1. Chapter 01

    The problem

    Fake tickets, delayed payouts and no visibility for organisers, especially smaller promoters and campus events.

  2. Chapter 02

    The build

    A platform designed mobile-first, with MoMo and card payments, QR validation and staff tools from day one.

  3. Chapter 03

    The launch

    Opened to organisers across Accra, then Kumasi, Takoradi, Cape Coast, Tamale and beyond.

  4. Chapter 04

    Today

    Thousands of events, a growing community, and a roadmap shaped by the organisers who use Mujtic every week.

Refund Policy

General Refund Policy

Refund eligibility is determined by the event organizer's policy set at the time of purchase. Mujtic facilitates but does not guarantee refunds.

Event Cancellations

If an organizer cancels an event, all ticket holders are entitled to a full refund. Mujtic will process these automatically within 5–7 business days.

Attendee-Initiated Refunds

Refund requests must be submitted through your Profile > Purchase History before the refund window closes.

Service Fees

If an organiser cancels an event, you receive a full refund of everything you paid, including any service fee added at checkout. For refunds you request yourself, a service fee added at checkout is not refundable; the ticket price is.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days depending on payment method.
